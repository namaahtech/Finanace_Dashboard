# MASTER CONSULTANT & NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

*This Master Consultant & Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of the date of electronic signature and portal onboarding completion (the "Effective Date").*

**BETWEEN:**
1. **NAMAAH PVT LTD**, an AI-Native Creative & Systems Company incorporated under the laws of India, having its principal place of operations at Bangalore, Karnataka (hereinafter referred to as the **"Company"**); and
2. **THE UNDERSIGNED CONSULTANT**, an individual whose credentials, identity, and personal details are verified and recorded upon dashboard onboarding initialization (hereinafter referred to as the **"Consultant"**).

---

### **RECITALS**
* **WHEREAS**, the Company designs, engineers, and operates advanced AI-native systems, cinematic content pipelines, design systems, and digital product experiences built for the AI era;
* **WHEREAS**, the Company desires to engage the Consultant to perform professional technical, creative, or engineering consulting services; and
* **WHEREAS**, in connection with such engagement, the Consultant will have access to, generate, or become acquainted with highly confidential, proprietary, and trade secret information of the Company.

**NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS MUTUALLY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:**

---

### **1. SERVICES AND ENGAGEMENT**
1.1 **Scope of Work:** The Consultant agrees to perform services for the Company, including but not limited to, software engineering, AI production pipeline optimization, creative systems design, database administration, and systems architecture (collectively, the **"Services"**).
1.2 **Standard of Performance:** The Consultant shall perform the Services in a professional, ethical, and workmanlike manner, conforming to the highest industry standards of the creative technology and AI engineering domains.
1.3 **Independent Contractor Status:** *The Consultant is engaged strictly as an independent contractor.* Nothing in this Agreement shall construct or imply a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employer-employee relationship between the parties.

---

### **2. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE**
2.1 **Definition of Confidential Information:** **"Confidential Information"** shall mean any and all technical, business, financial, or creative data disclosed by the Company to the Consultant, or otherwise acquired by the Consultant in the course of the engagement. This includes, without limitation:
* *Proprietary Source Code, Database Schemas, and Software Architecture.*
* *AI Model Weights, Hyperparameter Configurations, Training Datasets, and Fine-Tuning Protocols.*
* *Prompt Engineering Templates, Fine-Tuning Pipelines, and Custom Workflows.*
* *Cinematic Pre-productions, Creative Assets, Designs, and Brand Experiences.*
* *Customer Lists, Financial Projections, Pricing Metrics, and Strategic Marketing Plans.*

2.2 **Exclusions:** Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by the Consultant; or (b) was already in the Consultant's possession prior to disclosure, as verified by written records.
2.3 **Protection and Non-Use:** The Consultant agrees to hold all Confidential Information in the strictest confidence and shall not, directly or indirectly, use, copy, distribute, publish, or disclose any Confidential Information to third parties without the prior written consent of an authorized officer of the Company.

---

### **3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND PROPRIETARY RIGHTS**
3.1 **Ownership of Work Product:** The Consultant agrees that all work product, including but not limited to, source code, scripts, designs, prompt configurations, AI model fine-tunes, documentation, data models, and assets generated or conceived by the Consultant, solely or jointly, in the performance of the Services (the **"Work Product"**), shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.
3.2 **Assignment of Rights:** The Consultant hereby irrevocably and unconditionally assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to the Work Product, including all copyrights, patents, trade secrets, trademarks, database rights, and moral rights therein.
3.3 **Assistance:** The Consultant agrees to assist the Company, at the Company’s request and expense, to secure, register, and defend the Company's intellectual property rights in the Work Product in any jurisdiction worldwide.

---

### **4. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION**
4.1 **Restrictive Covenant:** During the term of this engagement and for a period of **twelve (12) months** immediately following its termination for any reason, the Consultant shall not directly or indirectly engage in, perform services for, or establish any business entity that competes directly with the specialized AI-native creative technology and systems development business of the Company.
4.2 **Non-Solicitation of Personnel:** The Consultant shall not, during the engagement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, solicit, induce, or attempt to influence any employee, consultant, or contractor of the Company to terminate their relationship with the Company.
4.3 **Non-Solicitation of Clients:** The Consultant shall not, during the engagement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, solicit or attempt to solicit the business of any client, customer, or partner of the Company for competing services.

---

### **5. TERM AND TERMINATION**
5.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in force until terminated as provided herein.
5.2 **Termination for Convenience:** Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing **thirty (30) days** prior written notice to the other party.
5.3 **Immediate Termination for Breach:** The Company reserves the right to terminate this Agreement immediately and without notice if the Consultant breaches any provision of Section 2 (Confidentiality) or Section 3 (Intellectual Property), or engages in any conduct that compromises the cybersecurity, integrity, or reputation of the Company.
5.4 **Survival:** The obligations of the Consultant under Sections 2, 3, 4, and 6 shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement.

---

### **6. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION**
6.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement, and all claims or disputes arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by, construed, and enforced in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of India**, without regard to conflict of law principles.
6.2 **Dispute Resolution:** In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of this Agreement, the parties shall first attempt to resolve the issue amicably through good-faith negotiation.
6.3 **Exclusive Jurisdiction:** *Any legal action, suit, or proceeding arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the courts located in Bangalore, Karnataka, India.*

---

7.1 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, or representations.
7.2 **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
